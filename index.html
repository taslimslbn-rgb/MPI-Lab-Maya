Tujuan Pembelajaran

1. Peserta didik mampu mengidentifikasi dan menganalisis motivasi tokoh (desire, fear, belief, need) dalam teks naratif berbahasa Inggris.

2. Peserta didik mampu membedakan dan menganalisis konflik internal (man vs self) dan konflik eksternal (man vs man, man vs nature, man vs society).

3. Peserta didik mampu mengidentifikasi latar belakang budaya dan nilai-nilai dalam folklore/karya sastra dunia.

4. Peserta didik mampu mengevaluasi nilai moral dan pelajaran sosial serta mengaitkan relevansinya dengan kehidupan nyata (relevance, critique, application).

Materi

Theoretical Foundation

Full Reading Text

The Fisherman's Debt

Arman was a young fisherman in a small coastal village. He longed to leave for the city, believing a better life waited beyond the horizon. Yet he feared abandoning his sick mother, who depended on him for everything. Arman believed that a son's duty was to protect his family above his own ambition, but he also needed to prove that he was more than just "the fisherman's son" the village always called him.

One night, a violent storm struck the coast. Waves swallowed the docks, and boats were thrown against the rocks. Arman rushed to save his own boat, his family's only source of income. On his way, he saw Dharma, his long-time rival, trapped beneath his capsized boat, screaming for help. Arman hesitated. If he saved Dharma, he might lose his own boat forever. If he ignored him, he could keep his family safe from poverty.

Torn between pride and compassion, Arman finally swam through the raging sea toward Dharma and pulled him to safety. His own boat was swept away by the storm. The next morning, the village elders, who once doubted Arman's judgment, praised his courage. Although he lost his boat, Arman gained something he had never expected: the respect and trust of his village.

Story Summary

The Fisherman's Debt

Arman longs to leave his coastal village for the city, torn between duty to his sick mother and his own ambition. When a storm hits, he must choose between saving his family's boat or rescuing his rival Dharma.

Character Dynamics & Conflict

The DFBN framework (Desire, Fear, Belief, Need) maps what drives a character's choices. Conflict can be internal (Man vs. Self — an inner struggle) or external (Man vs. Man, Man vs. Nature, or Man vs. Society).

DFBN in Arman

Desire: leave for the city. Fear: losing his mother. Belief: a son must protect family with integrity. Need: to prove he is more than "the fisherman's son."

Internal vs. External

Internal: Arman's hesitation between his boat and Dharma (Man vs. Self). External: fighting the raging sea to reach Dharma (Man vs. Nature).

Full Reading Text

The Weaver Who Wove the Moon

In a village nestled between the hills, an old tradition was passed down through generations of weavers. It was believed that every cloth carried the spirit of the ancestors who first taught the craft. Mira, a young weaver, was chosen to weave a ceremonial cloth for the village's harvest festival, a task considered a great honor and responsibility.

The village believed the pattern of the cloth had to be woven exactly as their ancestors had done, using natural dyes and threads spun by hand, or the festival's blessing would fail. But Mira had learned new techniques from travelers who once visited the village, techniques that made the weaving faster and the patterns more intricate.

Mira faced a dilemma: honor the old ways exactly as taught, or blend them with new methods she believed could make the tradition even more beautiful. She spoke with the village elders and listened to their stories about why each thread and color held meaning. In the end, Mira wove the ceremonial cloth using the traditional patterns and materials, but added a small, new detail at the border, a quiet tribute to both the past and the future. The elders approved, seeing that Mira had honored her ancestors while keeping the tradition alive for the next generation.

Story Summary

The Weaver Who Wove the Moon

Mira, a young weaver, must decide whether to follow her ancestors' ceremonial cloth pattern exactly, or blend it with new techniques she learned from travelers.

Cultural Background & Values

Cultural values in narrative are often shown through tradition, symbols, and community relationships. Stories can reveal how a society balances respect for ancestry with openness to change.

Tradition & Meaning

The ceremonial cloth carries the spirit of the ancestors and is believed to secure the festival's blessing.

Respectful Renewal

Mira keeps the traditional pattern but adds one new detail — honoring the past while keeping the tradition alive.

Full Reading Text

The Merchant's Two Roads

Beno was a traveling merchant known throughout the region for his fair prices. One day, while crossing a forest, he found a heavy pouch of gold coins dropped by another merchant. No one had seen him find it, and he could easily have kept the gold to expand his own business.

Beno remembered his father's words: "A single act of honesty is worth more than a lifetime of profit." He decided to search for the pouch's owner instead of continuing his usual trade route. After two days of searching, he found the owner, an old merchant named Rasyid, who had lost everything and was preparing to end his journey in despair.

Rasyid offered Beno half of the gold as a reward, but Beno refused, saying that returning what was not his was simply the right thing to do. Years later, Rasyid became a wealthy trader again and remembered Beno's honesty. When Beno faced a difficult year with poor harvests affecting his trade, Rasyid became his most loyal business partner and helped him recover. The story reminds readers that honesty, though it may not always bring immediate reward, builds trust that can last a lifetime.

Story Summary

The Merchant's Two Roads

Beno, a traveling merchant, finds a lost pouch of gold and must decide whether to keep it or search for its owner, Rasyid.

Evaluating Moral Values

A moral value can be evaluated through three lenses: Relevance (why it matters today), Critique (its limitations or bias), and Application (how it plays out in real life).

Relevance & Critique

Honesty is still valued today, though real life doesn't always reward it immediately — the story's optimism can be questioned.

Application

Rasyid's later loyalty shows how a value learned in a story can produce real consequences and lasting trust.

Guided Practice

Simulation: Deep Learning Steps

Each meeting follows three Deep Learning stages: Mindful, Meaningful, and Joyful Learning across 5 JP (225 menit), lengkap dengan alat/bahan dan rubrik penilaian.

Focus: Character Dynamics & Conflict · 5 JP (225 menit)

"The Fisherman's Debt"

1. Mindful Learning (±40 menit)

  • Baca teks secara mandiri selama 10 menit; garis bawahi kata yang menunjukkan perasaan Arman.
  • Tulis 3 kata kunci yang menggambarkan kepribadian Arman.
  • Diskusi berpasangan 5 menit membandingkan kata kunci yang dipilih.

2. Meaningful Learning (±110 menit)

  • Kerja kelompok (4 siswa): isi Character Motivation Map (DFBN) dengan bukti teks.
  • Isi Conflict Chart: tentukan konflik internal vs eksternal beserta kutipan pendukung.
  • Diskusi kelas: konflik mana yang paling sulit dihadapi Arman, dan mengapa?

3. Joyful Learning (±60 menit)

  • Hot-Seat Role Play: satu siswa duduk sebagai Arman, menjawab pertanyaan teman sekelas.
  • Rotasi peran hot-seat pada 3 sukarelawan berbeda.
  • Refleksi singkat: apa yang kamu pelajari dari sudut pandang Arman?

Alat & Bahan

Teks cerita, lembar DFBN Motivation Map, lembar Conflict Chart, kartu nama "Arman", kartu pertanyaan hot-seat.

Rubrik Penilaian

Ketepatan isi (40%) · Partisipasi diskusi (30%) · Kreativitas menjawab dalam Hot-Seat (30%).

Focus: Cultural Background & Values · 5 JP (225 menit)

"The Weaver Who Wove the Moon"

1. Mindful Learning (±40 menit)

  • Baca cermat, catat detail budaya: tradisi, simbol, kepercayaan yang muncul.
  • Tandai kata/frasa yang menunjukkan nilai komunitas dan penghormatan pada leluhur.
  • Curah pendapat singkat: tradisi apa yang kamu kenal yang mirip menenun kain seremonial ini?

2. Meaningful Learning (±110 menit)

  • Kerja kelompok isi Comparison Chart: nilai dalam cerita vs budaya sendiri siswa.
  • Diskusikan makna kain seremonial bagi masyarakat desa dalam cerita.
  • Gallery walk: bagikan temuan awal ke kelompok lain dan beri masukan.

3. Joyful Learning (±60 menit)

  • Presentasikan perbandingan budaya secara kreatif: poster, mind map, atau simulasi singkat.
  • Sesi tanya jawab antar kelompok setelah presentasi.

Alat & Bahan

Teks cerita, lembar Comparison Chart, kertas poster, spidol warna, contoh gambar kain tenun (opsional).

Rubrik Penilaian

Kedalaman analisis budaya (40%) · Kejelasan perbandingan (30%) · Kreativitas presentasi (30%).

Focus: Evaluating Moral Values · 5 JP (225 menit)

"The Merchant's Two Roads"

1. Mindful Learning (±30 menit)

  • Identifikasi pesan moral yang tersurat maupun tersirat dalam cerita.
  • Tulis pesan moral tersebut dalam satu kalimat sendiri.

2. Meaningful Learning (±120 menit)

  • Kerja kelompok isi Moral Evaluation Chart (Relevance-Critique-Application).
  • Mini debat: "Apakah kejujuran selalu berbuah manis?" — kelas dibagi tim pro & kontra.
  • Setiap tim menyusun 2 argumen utama disertai bukti dari teks atau pengalaman nyata.

3. Joyful Learning (±60 menit)

  • Tulis esai reflektif singkat: "A Lesson I Would Carry."
  • 2–3 siswa membacakan esainya secara sukarela di depan kelas.

Alat & Bahan

Teks cerita, lembar Moral Evaluation Chart, kertas esai reflektif, kartu argumen debat.

Rubrik Penilaian

Kedalaman evaluasi moral (40%) · Argumentasi debat (30%) · Orisinalitas refleksi (30%).

Kunci Jawaban — Aktivitas Simulasi

Kunci/contoh jawaban untuk lembar kerja pada tahap Meaningful & Joyful Learning di tab Simulasi (DFBN Map, Conflict Chart, Comparison Chart, Moral Evaluation Chart, dan contoh jawaban Hot-Seat/Debat).

Kunci: DFBN Motivation Map

Desire: pergi ke kota untuk hidup lebih baik.
Fear: kehilangan/meninggalkan ibunya yang sakit.
Belief: anak laki-laki wajib melindungi keluarga, tetapi rasa hormat sejati datang dari tindakan tanpa pamrih.
Need: membuktikan dirinya lebih dari sekadar "anak nelayan."

Kunci: Conflict Chart

Internal (Man vs Self): keraguan Arman antara menyelamatkan perahunya atau Dharma.
Eksternal (Man vs Nature): berjuang melawan badai/laut untuk mencapai Dharma.
Eksternal tambahan (Man vs Society): keraguan warga desa terhadap Arman sejak awal cerita.

Contoh Jawaban Hot-Seat Role Play

Q: Why did you choose to save Dharma instead of your own boat?
A: "In that moment, I realized a life mattered more than any boat. My father always said real strength is shown through compassion, not possessions."

Q: Do you regret losing your boat?
A: "At first it hurt, because the boat was my family's income. But the trust I gained from the village means more than what I lost."

Kunci: Comparison Chart

Nilai dalam cerita: kain seremonial dianggap membawa roh leluhur; pola dan bahan alami harus dijaga agar berkah festival tidak gagal.
Contoh perbandingan budaya siswa: tradisi menenun/membatik di Nusantara (mis. tenun ikat, songket, batik) yang juga sarat makna leluhur dan sering diwariskan turun-temurun. Jawaban siswa bersifat terbuka, dinilai dari kejelasan kaitan nilai, bukan kesamaan detail.

Kunci: Kriteria Presentasi

Presentasi yang baik menyebutkan: (1) nilai budaya dalam cerita, (2) nilai budaya pembanding dari siswa, (3) persamaan/perbedaan cara masyarakat menyeimbangkan tradisi dan perubahan — seperti yang dilakukan Mira dengan menambahkan detail baru tanpa mengubah pola inti.

Poin Kunci Diskusi

Mira tidak menolak tradisi maupun sepenuhnya menolak perubahan — ia menjaga pola dan bahan utama, lalu menambahkan satu detail baru di pinggir kain sebagai penghormatan pada masa depan. Ini menunjukkan bahwa inovasi yang dilakukan dengan hormat dapat menjaga budaya tetap hidup.

Kunci: Moral Evaluation Chart (Relevance-Critique-Application)

Relevance: kejujuran masih menjadi nilai penting dalam masyarakat modern, terutama dalam hubungan bisnis dan kepercayaan.
Critique: kehidupan nyata tidak selalu langsung memberi imbalan atas kejujuran — optimisme cerita ini bisa dipertanyakan.
Application: kesetiaan Rasyid di kemudian hari menunjukkan bagaimana nilai yang dipelajari dari cerita dapat membawa konsekuensi nyata dan hubungan jangka panjang.

Contoh Argumen Debat — Pro

Kejujuran membangun reputasi dan kepercayaan jangka panjang, seperti yang dialami Beno; dalam bisnis maupun pertemanan, hal ini sering berbuah manfaat di masa depan meski tidak instan.

Contoh Argumen Debat — Kontra

Dalam kenyataan, kejujuran tidak selalu dihargai atau dibalas; kerugian jangka pendek bisa sangat besar, dan cerita ini cenderung idealis dibanding realitas sehari-hari.
